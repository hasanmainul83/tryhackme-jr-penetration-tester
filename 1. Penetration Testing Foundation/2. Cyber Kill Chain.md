

Now comes the important stuff—the Cyber Kill Chain. There are 7 stages. Here they are.

- **Reconnaissance** 
    
- **Weaponization** 
    
- **Delivery** 
    
- **Exploitation**
    
- **Installation** 
    
- **Command & Control (C2)** 
    
- **Actions on Objectives** 




# The 7 Phases Explained in Simple Terms

1. **Look Around (Reconnaissance)** — You poke around the target, check out what's exposed, what they're running, and where you might find a way in.
    
2. **Build the Thing (Weaponization)** — You put together an exploit or payload that goes after the weak spot you found.
    
3. **Send It (Delivery)** — You get the payload to the target — email, link, USB, or whatever gets it in front of them.
    
4. **Break In (Exploitation)** — You trigger the weak spot and use it to get into the system.
    
5. **Stay In (Installation)** — You leave something behind that lets you keep access to the system, even after the initial break-in.
    
6. **Keep the Line Open (Command & Control)** — You set up a way to remotely talk to the machine you broke into — send commands, get responses back.
    
7. **Do What You Came For (Actions on Objectives)** — You go after the real goal: grab sensitive data, move deeper into the network, or whatever else you came to do.
